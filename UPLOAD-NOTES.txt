OTR - COMPLETE CURRENT PROJECT (version 67)

New in v67: multi-buy deals are simpler. A deal typed on a shape line (the
Hearts line under Price and stock) is now "any 2 hearts, any scent" by
itself - no group word to type, and the greyed-out example numbers that
looked filled in are gone. The product-level deal has a "Mix and match
with" choice (this product only / any <category> / a group of your own)
instead of a free-text word. Hearts deals already saved with no group word
are fixed up once on deploy (content version 14), so Michelle's two-scents
test should work straight after. The product list says "(this product
only)" on any deal that does not mix.

v66: a label that fits the back of the clamshell. The pack back is
about 78 x 70 mm, so the new default size is 72 x 63.5 mm on "12 per sheet,
63.5 x 72 mm" A4 stickers (Avery L7164 size) in the Canon; the label is
turned on its side on the sheet so a landscape label comes off an upright
sticker. Labels now shrink their own text (7 -> 6.5 -> 6 pt) when that is
the only way to fit, and the page lists which ones did. At 7 pt 51 of 59
fit as is, 7 more go to 6.5 or 6 pt, and Clean Cotton does not fit at all
and needs a fold-out from the Sample packs tab. 105 x 74 mm stays for bigger
packaging; the Phomemo rolls stay for symbol-free labels.

v65: the label preview shrinks to fit a phone screen instead of
cutting the left edge off (it says "Shrunk to 72%"; printing is unaffected).

v64: a 105 x 74 mm size for A4 sticker sheets (8 per sheet, 2 x 4,
no gaps) printed on the colour inkjet. It is the default: every fragrance
fits at 8 pt, the warning diamonds print red, and the size is over the CLP
minimum. The print puts each label exactly on its sticker, with the nudge
boxes back for a printer that is a millimetre out. The three Phomemo roll
sizes stay for symbol-free labels.

v63: the CLP label maker is set up for the Phomemo M110 label
printer. The label sizes are now just its three rolls - 50 x 80, 50 x 30 and
40 x 30 mm - and the old A4 / Avery sticker-sheet sizes, the gap control and
the nudge boxes are gone. Printing sends one label per page at the roll size,
so the printer feeds one sticker per label; choose the Phomemo and the matching
paper size in the print box, 100% scale. All 36 fragrances without a warning
symbol fit the 50 x 80 roll at 7 pt.

Important: the Phomemo prints black only, and CLP requires the diamond around
a warning symbol to be RED. So the 23 fragrances with a symbol cannot be
properly labelled on it - the page says so on each one and lists them under
Print labels. Those need a colour printer or pre-printed red-diamond labels.

v62: a yellow "Leave a review" button at the bottom of the phone
menu. Paste the Google or Facebook review link into Admin -> Delivery &
settings -> Business details -> "Leave a review link" and the button opens
it in a new tab; until then it goes to the Contact page.

v61: the Delivery & returns page gets a "Local delivery and
collection" section (the £4 / 10 miles / times, and the Sutton Way address
and Sunday morning collection), slotted in before "Dispatch and delivery
times" by content migration 13. It is added once; Michelle can edit it under
Pages like the rest of the page.

v60: local delivery and collection. Checkout now asks "How would you
like your order?": post (as before), Local delivery (£4, within 10 miles of
CH65 7DN, on Sunday 12.30pm to 2pm, Tuesday 6pm to 8pm or Thursday 6pm to
8pm) or Collection (free, from 18 Sutton Way, Whitby, Ellesmere Port, Sunday
10am to 12pm). The customer picks a time. For local delivery the distance
from the shop postcode to theirs is checked (postcodes.io, free, no key) and
anyone further than 10 miles is asked to choose post. Collection takes a name
and phone number only, no address. Orders, emails, the thank-you page and the
packing slip all say which one and the time. Everything is editable or can
be switched off in Admin -> Delivery & settings. Three columns on orders add
themselves on first request; the times and address are put in once by the
content migration (version 12) and never overwrite later edits.

v59: admin Products can sort by price, low to high or high to low
(a product counts as its cheapest option; unpriced ones go last).

v58: Position now works the way you would expect. Products given a
Position of 1, 2, 3, 4 come first in that order; products left at 0 have no
position and follow on after them. Before, 0 counted as "first", so numbering a
product 1 pushed it behind everything that had never been numbered.

v57: a calmer desktop menu. The bar is Shop, Seasonal, Blog, Contact
and the basket; Shop drops down the seven picture tiles with an All products
link. The Home pill is gone (the logo is Home). Phones are unchanged.

v55: Broken Biscuits tile uses the new heap photo, cut out on a clear
background like the other categories. v53: a Broken Biscuits category - cracked, chipped and broken melts
at a lower price. It is in the admin Category list, the shop menu tiles on
every page, the homepage "Shop by type" row, the shop filters and the
sitemap, with its own photo. public/seasonal.html is gone from the repo as
the notes always asked (the /seasonal page is served by a function).

v52: the product form lays out properly on a phone - option rows and
deal lines stack instead of squashing into four columns. v51: admin Products opens sorted by Recently added. v50: shows when each product was last changed, sorts
by Recently changed / Recently added, and has a "Changed this week" chip.

v49: each shape has its own price and stock. Tick the shapes a snap
bar comes in under Category, and a price row appears for each under Price and
stock (the existing price moves to the first shape). Customers pick the shape
on the product page and pay that shape's price; the shop card says "From £x"
and "Choose shape". One column on variants adds itself on first request.

v48: customers pick the shape. A snap bar with shapes ticked shows a
Shape choice on its product page (and "Choose shape" on the shop card instead
of Add to Basket). Hearts and squares of one scent sit on separate basket
lines, share the scent's stock, and the shape prints on the order, the
packing slip, the emails and the Stripe line.

v47: Shape tick boxes under Category for snap bars (Hearts, 5 piece snap
bar, Square, Circle) - tick every shape the scent comes in. The Snap Bars page in the shop gets a "Filter by shape"
row showing only the shapes on sale, and the admin product list can filter
by shape too. One column on products adds itself on first request.

v45: shape options, and deals per shape. On a product, "Use shapes: Square,
Heart, Rectangle" sets up a Shape option with the three, each with its own
price and stock (a heart weighs less, so it can cost less). Each option now
has its own deal line, so "hearts 2 for £5" works when Heart is a shape inside
every scent: put the group word on the Heart line of each scent and the deal
runs across them, while squares stay full price. Three columns on variants
add themselves on first request.

v44: a Category filter on the admin product list.

v43: multi-buy deals. Admin -> Products -> open a product -> Multi-buy
deal: a group word (e.g. hearts), a quantity (2) and a price (5.00). Every
product with the same group word is in the same deal, mix and match, and
saving one updates the rest. Customers see "Any 2 hearts for £5" on the
product, the shop cards and the basket, with "add 1 more" nudges; the saving
comes off at checkout and shows on the Stripe payment page as its own line.
Odd ones out are charged at full price. Two new columns on products and two
on orders add themselves on the first request after deploy.

v42: the sticker-sheet sizes named the way the box names them.

v41: pre-cut label sheets. The label size list now has 63.5 x 38.1 mm
(Avery L7160 / "63 x 38" multipurpose, 21 per sheet) and 99.1 x 67.7 mm
(L7165, 8 per sheet), and the print sheet puts each label exactly on its
sticker, with nudge boxes for a printer that is a millimetre out. Wide labels
get a landscape layout. Any label whose wording does not fit is now left off
the print sheet and listed in red, instead of printing with the end cut off.
Both sizes are under the 52 x 74 mm CLP minimum; at 7 pt, 28 of the 59
fragrances fit the small one and none of the ones with a symbol do.

v40: sample packs. Admin -> CLP labels -> Sample packs lays out a
label for a clamshell holding several different melts, as a fold-out strip:
the front panel has the pack name and business details, then every fragrance
gets its own numbered block with its own wording. The blocks are never merged
into one list. It measures how many panels a pack needs, warns when a strip is
too wide for A4, and prints on landscape A4 with the fold lines marked.
Everything from version 40 is included.
