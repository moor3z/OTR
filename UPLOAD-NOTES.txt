OTR - COMPLETE CURRENT PROJECT (version 39)

New in v39: the CLP label maker is now part of the shop admin, at
Admin -> CLP labels. It lays out the safety labels that legally have to go on
each melt and prints them a sheet at a time. All 59 fragrances are already in
it, taken from FizzyWhiz's own CLP sheets for each oil at 10% in a
non-hazardous base. Everything from version 38 is included.

It sits behind the same Cloudflare Access sign-in as the rest of the admin
area, and saves to the shop's own database, so Michelle sees the same list on
her phone and on the computer.
